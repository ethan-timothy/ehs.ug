Extreme High School is not a wealthy school; we depend on donations from
volunteers, well-wishers and child sponsorship to educate and care for
children who would otherwise go without.

Ways you can help:

- [Supporting Us](supporting-us/) - donations, bank details and how funds
  are used
- [Sponsoring a Child](sponsoring-a-child/) - sponsor a child's education
  from $200 a term
- [Projects](projects/) - help us finish the examination hall
