***FINISH BUILDING THE EXAMINATION CENTRE***

![](/images/hall.jpg)

**The examination hall was built up to the wall plate as you can see in the picture above and its ready for roofing.**

**Building the main hall is a viable solution for this problem:
• We shall be able to have the Uganda National Examination centre and this will reduce the expenses of paying registration fees for senior four students**

**• It will be a source of income since students from schools will be paying to use the centre**

• It will be a foundation of income for the school since community members will hire it for parties and wedding ceremonies
• The main building will be used as a general school meeting centre ..
