Introduction and History

Extreme
High School is located along Hoima road in
the central part of Uganda . The school was founded by Segawa Ephraim
supported by international volunteers from America, Canada, Austria and Australia
and UK

Today
the school has been constructed in a modern and well-equipped with student population of approx. 250 (from
senior one to six).

Objectives of the School

To
provide an all-round education for students in terms of mental alertness,
intellectual capability, emotional balance, forthright morality, spiritual
awareness and physical fitness.

To
inspire creativity among students and encourage them to discover their innate
talents and abilities through a
pleasurable and adventurous learning process which ultimately leads them to
their goals.

To
inculcate practical and objective thinking in students, with a sense of
discrimination and purpose towards their actions and to weigh and consider the
outcomes with reasons, empathy and intuitive judgment.

To
train students to be self-reliant, resourceful and effectively manage the
challenges of life while harmoniously adapting to constant changes in the
environment with self confidence, general awareness, self control and mature
symbiotic relationships.

To
instill human values in students and to guide them to become men and women of
character and to act as agents of positive change in society; towards creating
a better future and a higher standard of life.

To
uphold the principles of ‘learn to live, learn to do, learn to be & learn
to know’ in order to acquire an integrated personality.

To
stress on quality education based on the ‘Multiple Intelligence Mode’ offering
a variety of subjects taught in English to cater to the varying capabilities of
students; to enable them to discover their aptitudes and to develop a global
perspective.
