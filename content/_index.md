Extreme High School is a mixed day and boarding school located in Namayumba
town council, Wakiso district, on Hoima road in Uganda. The school has a
student population of 250, 14 teachers, 6 technical staff and more than 10
volunteer staff a year from western countries.

## History

Extreme High School was founded in 2010 by Segawa Ephraim, growing out of
Nansana Community primary school which he started in 2004 as an informal home
school teaching reading and writing to orphans and other community vulnerable
children. The number of students keeps growing, with students coming from as
far as Kasese, Busoga, Toro, Ankole and Bunyoro.

## Mission Statement

The mission statement of *EHS* is to nurture the "whole child" in areas of
physical, intellectual, emotional, social and spiritual development. This will
be achieved through providing a variety of activities that are developmentally
appropriate and recognize the individual needs and differences of children.
Through our principles and teacher-assisted and directed activities, children
are encouraged to be independent thinkers and life-long learners.

EHS provides and teaches students studies in the arts and sciences found at
institutions of secondary learning. A high school-based curriculum tailored
specifically for students, taught in a fun, nurturing care giving environment.
