**1 Billion children live in poverty throughout the world! Most people agree
that this is wrong. Most people would like to see that change. We want to
invite you to be that change.**

We are currently supporting over 800 children in Wakiso and Kiboga districts
of Uganda. We provide them education from nursery school, primary school,
secondary school and vocational training. Our education program is designed to
nurture and educate children, helping them to reach their God-given potential.

We want to encourage Ugandans to help other Ugandans; together working towards
a sustainable future by looking into our own resources to help the needy in
our midst.

To address such challenges, we have therefore organized a music gala
fundraiser to be held at Adventist Centre Hall (opposite Law Development
Centre, Makerere) on the 24th February 2013 from 2.00 to 7.00 pm, intending to
raise money:

- To roof the examination hall, which has been built up to the wall plate
- To register 35 (thirty five) S4 candidates 2013 - children without parents,
  who have been supported by CCALP Uganda from nursery to senior four
- To buy single sitter writing desks so that the 35 (thirty five) S4
  candidates 2013 sit UNEB exams in our school centre at Namayumba

The choral group to perform in this gala will be *Hope International
Children's Choir, Young Cranes Artist, Keno Musanyusa and Pastor Wilson
Bugembe*. Their performances will be combined of Christian and cultural
African standard songs, poems, dances and drama. The choirs have had frequent
performances and have recorded audio and video songs to be sold at the event.

We kindly request your board's contributions, by selling tickets to your
staff members at 100,000 Golden, 50,000 Silver, 20,000 Diamond and 10,000
Bronze.

We are looking forward to meeting your board members at your favorable time.
If you have questions that need to be clarified, please don't hesitate to ask.

Thank you and with our heartfelt regards.

Very truly yours,

**Segawa Ephraim**
