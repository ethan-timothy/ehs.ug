Extreme High School Namayumba is a mixed day and boarding school for
senior one to senior six. We keep class sizes small - in most cases less
than 40 - so teachers know their students well and can monitor their
progress with care.

Everything you need to join us:

- [Admission](admission/) - how to apply, class sizes and waiting list
- [Curriculum](curriculum/) - extracurricular activities, community
  service and excursions
- [Vacancies](vacancies/) - school fees, uniform, requirements and term
  dates
