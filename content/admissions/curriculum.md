**EXTRACURRICULAR**

**AFTER SCHOOL ACTIVITIES**

Through
our program of extracurricular activities students learn new skills and take on
leadership roles. Students may choose from a variety of activities from sports
to crafts. Most activities are conducted by the teaching staff. Talented people
from our community and professional coaches help to coordinate and run some of
the activities and sports. Examples of activities include chess, indoor games,
nature club, drama, band, student newspaper, ball games. Activities/Sport
afternoons are Wednesdays and Fridays.
The school soccer and basketball teams train regularly for inter and intra
school competitions.
Music teachers offer tuition in piano, guitar, drums and other string
instruments.

**COMMUNITY SERVICE**

All
EHSN students are encouraged to participate in community service activities
through the course of the school year. The aim is for students to experience
and develop respect toward others, an active appreciation for the environment,
an awareness of community at different levels and growth in interpersonal and
social maturity.

**EXCURSIONS**

EHSN recognizes the value of field trips when used to
supplement other curriculum materials. They also provide memorable, unifying
experiences for students. Students from across the school are provided with a
variety of trips to enrich their whole school experience, both within our local
area and beyond. Adequate supervision and safety are our top priority.
