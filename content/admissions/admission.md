Page Title

Admissions

The school maintains
 a policy of keeping class sizes small, in most cases less than 40. This
 allows the students to gain a more
 personal relationship with their teachers at the same time as enabling the
 teachers to know their students well
 and to devote more time to individuals and to monitor their progress with
 care.

How to Apply

To apply for
 admission to Extreme College School, fill in our ***application form*** (boarding
 applicants should fill in the ***boarding* *application*** form as well). Application form can be found
 Extreme college office or at Nansana Community school and soon will be
 available on Internet

 Please note, because of demand for places, it is advisable to make early
 arrangements to enroll your child at Extreme College School. In the case of a
 class being full, your child will only be placed on the waiting list on
 payment of a non-refundable deposit of 20,000/ Uganda shillings
